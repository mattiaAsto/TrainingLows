Common answers

Frequently asked questions

I changed my email. Why can I not use the account yet?

Changing your email starts a new verification cycle. Open the verification link sent to the new address. When you are signed in, it returns you to Settings.

How does an athlete connect to a trainer?

The trainer sends an invitation using the athlete email and the athlete's registered birth date. The athlete must review and accept the invitation before the roster connection is created.

What is the difference between a plan and a session?

A season plan describes objectives, phases, and targets. A session is one planned or completed workout placed on the calendar.

Why is a Strava activity waiting for review?

TrainingLows keeps imported activities pending when manual review is enabled. Open Strava settings to accept or reject each activity before it enters your completed training.

Will adding an activity type delete existing training data?

No. Activity types use the shared activities table. Updating the catalog and restarting the application does not remove existing users, sessions, or plans.

Why does a season timeline extend beyond December?

The planning horizon ends at the latest planned objective when that objective falls after the calendar year. Otherwise it ends on December 31.
